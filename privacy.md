# Privacy policy

This application ("Frank") is operated by its owner for personal use only.

It accesses the owner's own Gmail mailbox (read-only) and the owner's own Google Sheets in order to record short-term-rental bookings and management invoices for a single property. Processing happens on the owner's own computer.

No data belonging to any other person is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone. No data is transferred to third parties. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.
